# Bike-Sales-Dashboard-Excel
🚲 Bike Sales Dashboard (Excel)
📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Bike Sales Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel to analyze customer demographics, income levels, and commuting distances that influence bike purchasing decisions. The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful insights using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers.
## Dataset Used
- <a href = "https://github.com/Shaban-k7/Bike-Sales-Dashboard-Excel/blob/main/MYProject(Working%20Sheet).csv"> Dataset View </a>

🎯 Objectives
Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
Identify key demographic groups driving bike sales.
Examine the relationship between income and bike purchases.
Understand how commuting distance affects purchasing decisions.
Support data-driven business decisions through visual analytics.

📊 Dashboard Insights
1. Age Brackets vs Purchased Bike
Middle-aged customers represent the largest share of bike purchasers.
Adolescents and older adults show comparatively lower purchase rates.
Middle-aged individuals appear to be the most profitable customer segment.
2. Purchased Bike vs Distance
Customers traveling shorter distances (0–1 miles) have the highest bike purchase rates.
Purchase likelihood generally declines as commuting distance increases.
Long-distance commuters are less likely to purchase bikes.
3. Average Income Per Purchase
Bike purchasers have higher average incomes than non-purchasers.
Male customers show higher average income levels than female customers.
Income appears to be a significant factor influencing bike purchases.
